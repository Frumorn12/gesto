First release as Gesto, formerly Waypad: new name, new look, and a home with your PCs that reopens on the last PC and mode. Add a PC by confirming a 4-digit PIN on the PC, no code to type. The screen comes as hardware H.264 with the desktop's sound, a quick menu, direct touch, zoom and game mode. Start apps on the PC, wake it when it's off. Light and dark theme, English and Italian. It installs as a new app: add your PC again, then remove Waypad.
